# Commercial Satellite Data Acquisition Program Resources
## Overview of this Repo
This GitHub repository contains resources developed by the CSDA (Commercial Satellite Data Acquisition) team and contributions from users of commercial data. These resources were developed to support access to and use of CSDA data and may be of value to the wider scientific community. Community feedback and comments on these resources are encouraged and welcomed. Either individual authors or the CSDA team will be identified as the author of each resource. 

## Contribute
If you would like to contribute a tool, please make a pull request to include it in the repo. The tool must be developed for commercial data products available through the [CSDA Program](https://csdap.earthdata.nasa.gov/). Author name, author affiliation, data used (vendor, product, dates, image IDs preferred) must be included in an overview section. The code will be reviewed, but not tested, by the CSDA data team before inclusion in this repo. CSDA is not responsible for the results of using contributions from external authors. 

## Support
You may open an issue if you encounter a bug or have a question about a specific resource. You may also reach out to the CSDA Operations team at csda-support@nasa.gov with any questions. 

## The CSDA Program
The [Commercial Satellite Data Acquisition (CSDA) program](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/about/csda) was established by NASA's Earth Science Division (ESD) to identify, evaluate, and acquire commercial small-satellite (smallsat) data that support NASA's Earth science research and application goals. These commercial satellite data may provide a cost-effective way to augment or complement the suite of Earth observations acquired by NASA, other U.S. Government agencies, and international partners. ESD recognizes the potential impact commercial satellite constellations may have in encouraging/enabling efficient approaches to advancing Earth System Science and applications development for societal benefit.

The objectives of the program are to:

* Establish continuous and repeatable processes to onramp new commercial data vendors and evaluate data for its potential to advance NASA's Earth science research and applications activities.
* Enable the sustained use of purchased data for broader use and dissemination by NASA scientific community.
* Ensure long-term data preservation through the establishment of data management processes and systems to support rapid evaluation, access and distribution of purchased data, and long-term access to purchased data for scientific reproducibility.
* Coordinate with other U.S. Government agencies and international partners on the evaluation and scientific use of commercial data.
